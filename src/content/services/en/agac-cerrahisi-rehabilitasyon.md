Just like humans, trees can live, get injured, and fall ill. Tree surgery carried out by our ISA-certified arborists is not merely a visual correction — it is an advanced rehabilitation process aimed at preserving your tree's life cycle, halting decay, and restoring structural integrity.

## When Is Surgical Intervention Required?

- **Cavity & Decay Management:** Cleaning decayed areas (cavities) in the trunk using specialist techniques, and supporting the tree's own healing (compartmentalisation) process.
- **Wound Care:** Cleaning large breaks, bark losses, or injuries caused by external factors in a way that accelerates the tree's tissue regeneration.
- **Pest & Disease Isolation:** Isolating diseased tissue (fungal, bacterial, etc.) from surrounding healthy tissue using precise surgical methods to prevent spread.
- **Structural Support:** Integrating support systems into the tree's weak points.

## How Does the Process Work?

1. **Detailed Diagnosis:** Our arborists examine the tree's vitality, depth of decay, and recovery potential. Where necessary, the tree's internal structure is analysed using technological imaging methods.

2. **Hygienic Intervention:** All of our surgical equipment is sterilised with specialist disinfectants before and after each intervention to prevent the spread of disease.

3. **Tissue Cleaning & Protection:** Dead, decayed, or infected tissue is carefully removed without damaging living tissue. The tree's wounds are then treated in a way that supports its natural defence mechanism (the CODIT model).

4. **Recovery Monitoring:** We create a care programme to track the tree's development following intervention. The success of the surgery is directly linked to how effectively the tree "closes" its own wounds.

## Why Choose Us?

- **Preservation-Focused:** Cutting a tree down is the easy option; our goal is to extend your tree's lifespan through professional intervention.
- **Scientific Foundation:** With our ISA-certified expertise, we act with knowledge of the tree's anatomy and natural defence systems.
- **Modern Techniques:** We use the most current rehabilitation methods recognised worldwide by ISA Arbor.
- **Long-Term Follow-Up:** After intervention, we periodically check your tree's health, aiming for full recovery.
