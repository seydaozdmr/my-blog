Trees are not only aesthetic or shade-giving features — they can also be living spaces you interact with directly. Our tree hammock service is a bespoke installation process carried out under the supervision of ISA-certified arborists, causing no permanent damage to the tree's biological structure.

## Why Is Expert Installation Required?

- **Tree Health Comes First:** Incorrect mounting methods can cause permanent damage to the tree's bark, cambium tissue, and vascular system. Expert installation uses suspension systems and protective specialist clamps suited to the tree's anatomy.
- **Load & Balance Calculation:** For safe hammock installation, the branch diameter, angle, root structure, and the tree's overall static balance are evaluated by an arborist.
- **Aesthetics & Harmony:** The hammock position is planned so as not to distort the tree's form and to be in harmony with the overall appearance of your garden.

## Installation Process

1. **Tree Health & Structural Assessment:** It is examined whether the selected tree(s) can bear the hammock load, including branch health and root stability.
2. **System Selection:** The most suitable protective suspension system (Tree Saver strap, dynamic rope, etc.) is determined according to the tree's species and diameter.
3. **Mounting:** Installation is completed using protective edge-padded straps and certified hardware that ensure minimum contact with the tree's bark.
4. **Safety Test & Handover:** A load test is carried out after installation; usage instructions and periodic inspection recommendations are shared.

## Why Choose Us?

- **Tree-Friendly Mounting:** We install systems that use no nails, screws, or cutting tools, supporting the tree's natural growth.
- **ISA Arborist Approved:** Every installation is evaluated and approved from a tree health perspective.
- **Long-Lasting & Safe:** A hammock installed with the correct system protects both you and your tree for years.
- **Custom Design:** We offer different options ranging from single-person hammocks to wide family platforms.
