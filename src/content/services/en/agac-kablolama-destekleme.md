Sometimes even trees that appear majestic and healthy can pose a risk during storms or high winds due to structural weaknesses or weight imbalances. Our tree cabling service is a "support" system we apply to extend the life of your tree and prevent it from becoming a hazard to surrounding structures.

## What Is Tree Cabling and Why Is It Needed?

As trees age or develop incorrectly, tension can build up between branches. Our arborists identify the structural weaknesses in your tree and provide engineering solutions that keep it alive rather than having it removed. Cabling is a protective method that allows the tree its natural movement while preventing weak areas from breaking under excessive load.

## How Does the Process Work?

1. **Structural Assessment:** Our expert arborist inspects the tree's canopy and trunk union points in detail. Critical weaknesses (cracks, narrow-angled co-dominant stems, etc.) are identified.

2. **Strategic Installation:** Specialist flexible cabling systems are installed that do not damage the tree's anatomy or restrict its growth, but distribute the load effectively.

3. **Safety Inspection:** The installed support system is checked for correct tension. As the tree sways in the wind, this system shares the load and prevents branch failure.

4. **Monitoring & Periodic Checks:** Trees are living organisms — they grow over time. After installation, we create periodic maintenance plans to monitor the system's compatibility with the tree as it develops.

## Why Choose Us?

- **Tree-First Approach:** Rather than removing the tree, we keep it safely in place with the necessary interventions.
- **Arborist Knowledge:** We use techniques that comply with standards (ISA Arbor) and do not damage the tree's biological tissue.
- **Property Safety:** We professionally eliminate the hazards that branches above your home or garden could create.
- **Long-Term Solution:** Without interfering with the tree's natural form, we ensure it grows more safely and in better balance.
