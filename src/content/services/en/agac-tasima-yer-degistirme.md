Moving a tree is not simply a matter of uprooting it and replanting it elsewhere — it is a "life transplant" process. To ensure your trees adapt to their new location without compromising their form or health, we apply scientific methods with our ISA (International Society of Arboriculture) certified specialists.

## How Does the Process Work?

1. **On-Site Analysis & Planning:** The tree's species, root structure, soil conditions, and seasonal data are examined. The tree's physical condition is assessed by our arborists prior to transplanting.

2. **Root Preparation & Transport:** The tree's root system is excavated using specialist techniques (root ball preparation) to minimise damage, and is protected to maintain moisture balance during transport.

3. **Consultation & Project Planning:** Every tree and every location is different. We provide project planning and pre/post-transplant technical consultancy tailored to the soil composition, sunlight, and water requirements of the tree's new location.

4. **Planting & Adaptation:** The tree's placement at its new site, its growth orientation, and root stabilisation are carried out in accordance with ANSI A300 standards.

## Success Guarantee: 90% Survival Commitment

Thanks to the specialist care protocols we apply to minimise transplant stress, we have great confidence in your tree's ability to thrive in its new location. At our clients' request, we undertake professional aftercare for 2 years following transplanting. During this period, we monitor all of the tree's needs, ensure successful adaptation, and provide a **90% survival guarantee**.
