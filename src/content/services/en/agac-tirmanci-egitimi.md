Tree climbing is a discipline requiring expertise that can be extremely dangerous without the correct technique and equipment. This training programme, to be delivered by our ISA-certified arborists, will open its doors to everyone from forestry workers to hobby climbers, landscape professionals to curious adults.

## Training Scope

- **Basic Rope Techniques:** Theoretical and practical instruction in SRT (Single Rope Technique) and DRT (Double Rope Technique) systems.
- **Equipment Introduction & Safe Use:** Hands-on practice exclusively with certified climbing equipment (saddle, ascender, descender, carabiner, etc.).
- **Movement in the Canopy:** Safe positioning within the tree canopy, load transfer, and balance techniques.
- **Health & Safety and Risk Management:** Pre-work tree health assessment, environmental safety, and emergency procedures.
- **ISA Arbor Standards:** Safe climbing practices in accordance with international arboriculture norms.

## Who Can Participate?

Individuals aged 18 and over with basic physical fitness can participate in the training. Advanced modules are also planned for those pursuing professional development.

## Why Choose Us?

- **Real Field Experience:** Training is conducted directly on trees, not in a classroom.
- **ISA-Certified Instructors:** One-to-one learning opportunity from specialist, actively practising arborists.
- **Safety First:** All equipment is periodically inspected; an individual safety assessment is carried out for each participant.
- **Certificate:** A certificate of participation is planned to be awarded upon completion of the programme.

Follow the programme launch to forge a new connection with trees, pursue a career in this field, or simply experience this unique adventure.
