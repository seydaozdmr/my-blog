Unlike other trees, palm trees have a single growth centre (apex). Therefore, palm tree pruning is not just about removing dried fronds — it is about safeguarding the tree's biological health while preserving its aesthetics. Our ISA-certified team manages a meticulous pruning process with deep knowledge of each palm species' unique needs.

## Why Expert Palm Care?

- **Biological Sensitivity:** Incorrect (excessive or off-season) pruning can cut off the palm's nutrient sources or leave it vulnerable to disease and pests.
- **Red Palm Weevil Control:** We perform checks during pruning against the most significant pest threat in our region, the red palm weevil (*Rhynchophorus ferrugineus*), ensuring your tree remains resilient.
- **Aesthetic Form:** We meticulously apply "combing" and "trunk cleaning" techniques to reveal the palm's natural, majestic, and clean form.

## How Does the Process Work?

1. **Species Analysis:** We determine the most suitable pruning period and technique based on the palm species (Phoenix, Washingtonia, etc.).

2. **Hygienic Intervention:** We sterilise our pruning equipment after each tree — a vital safety step that prevents diseases from spreading from one tree to another.

3. **Dry Frond & Fruit Cluster Removal:** Dried fronds and unsightly fruit clusters are removed without damaging the tree trunk, in full compliance with ISA Arbor standards.

4. **Safety:** Our expert arborists complete the operation with all necessary safety measures for high-altitude work, without causing any damage to the surroundings.

## Why Choose Us?

- **ISA-Certified Expertise:** We work with specialists who have in-depth knowledge of palm anatomy and care requirements.
- **Disease Protection:** We make early diagnoses of potential disease symptoms during pruning.
- **Clean & Tidy:** We quickly clear the enormous frond piles that result from pruning, leaving your garden fresh and aesthetically pleasing.
- **Correct Timing:** We intervene at the healthiest periods without disrupting the palm's growth cycle.
